# Testers Area Community Guidelines & Fair Play Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** September 11, 2026  
**Version:** 2.0 (Production Release)  
**Platform:** Testers Area  
**Ecosystem:** Google Play 14-Day Closed Testing & Peer Verification  

---

## 1. Our Mission & Philosophy
Testers Area was established by independent Android developers for independent Android developers. Our mission is to help creators satisfy Google Play's 14-day closed testing requirement (20 testers for 14 continuous days) through **honest, peer-to-peer testing**, mutual developer respect, and genuine feedback.

We believe every indie developer deserves a level playing field without being forced to pay exorbitant third-party agency fees. In exchange, every community member commits to testing in good faith.

---

## 2. The 5 Golden Rules of Fair Testing

### 2.1 The 14-Day Keep-Installed Commitment
- When you accept a swap request, you commit to keeping the partner's app installed on your physical Android device for the **entire 14 days**.
- **Early Uninstallation is Fraud:** Uninstalling a partner's app while expecting your app to be tested is treated as bad-faith fraudulent behavior. The test will be immediately marked as failed fraud, and your Reputation Score will be penalized.

### 2.2 Active Daily Usage (Minimum 2 Minutes)
- You must launch and actively interact with the partner's app each day.
- Android Usage Statistics natively track foreground active usage duration. Running the app in the background or immediately closing it does not satisfy the daily requirement.
- Bots, auto-clickers, accessibility scripts, emulators, and virtual farms are strictly prohibited.

### 2.3 Genuine Daily Screenshot Proofs
- Submit two freshly captured, unedited screenshots showing the app running in foreground during your daily test.
- Reusing previous screenshots, uploading blank screens, or altering timestamps is automatically detected by our EXIF verification engine and results in proof rejection.

### 2.4 Respect for Paid Apps & Test Credentials
- **Paid Apps:** Developers listing paid apps must supply valid Google Play Promo Codes or configure a temporary $0 sale so testers can install completely free of charge.
- **Credential Protection:** If a developer provides demo login credentials (username/password or test OTP) in their Access Notes, testers must treat them with strict confidentiality.
- **Zero Abuse:** Never change passwords, modify account settings, or use promo codes on unauthorized secondary devices.

### 2.5 Constructive & Professional Feedback
- Testing is meant to uncover bugs, crashes, UI flaws, and localization errors before launching to production.
- Leave helpful, specific observations in the feedback panel (e.g., *"Crashed when opening Settings on Android 14"*, *"Text overlaps on small screens"*).
- Generic spam comments (e.g., *"nice"*, *"good app"*, *"done"*) do not help developers improve and may lead to feedback moderation.

## 3. Fraud Prevention, Reputation & Account Accountability

Testers Area maintains platform integrity through our **Reputation Score** and **Peer Accountability Framework**:

### 3.1 Reputation Score & Golden Points
- **Honest Testing Rewards:** Completing honest daily testing and verifying daily proofs increases your public Reputation Score and awards Golden Points upon 14-day completion.
- **Reputation Penalties:** Engaging in uninstallation fraud, submitting fake screenshots, or missing testing days deducts Reputation Score points and lowers your app matching priority.

### 3.2 Block App Pair & Reporting
- If a partner uninstalls your app or acts in bad faith, the test is automatically flagged as `failed_fraud`.
- You can immediately choose to **"Block App Pair"**, ensuring your application and their application will never be paired together again.
- You can submit a direct **Report** with details for administrative review.

### 3.3 Zero-Tolerance Ban Offenses
Accounts found engaging in the following harmful actions face immediate, permanent hardware-level account termination and forfeiture of all virtual coins:
- Submitting malware, spyware, adware, phishing links, or unauthorized mining scripts.
- Multi-accounting: Creating duplicate accounts on the same or multiple devices to manipulate queues or farm referral rewards.
- Harassment, threatening language, hate speech, or commercial spam in the developer partner chat.
- Clock tampering or spoofing NTP time to artificially bypass testing schedules.

---

## 4. Automated 48-Hour Auto-Approval Rule

To prevent unresponsive app owners from holding diligent testers hostage:
- App owners have **48 hours** to review daily proof submissions.
- If the owner fails to approve or dispute the proof within 48 hours, **our automated engine automatically approves the submission**, awarding the tester their streak credit and daily reputation score.

---

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Appeals

If you believe a testing proof was unfairly rejected:
1. Contact your testing partner through the in-app chat to clarify the issue (e.g., missing screenshot, wrong screen).
2. If unresolved, submit a dispute via **Settings > Send Feedback** with your request ID and screenshots.
3. Our administrative team reviews the raw Android Usage Statistics telemetry and timestamp logs to make a final, binding determination.

---

## 6. Rewards Hub & Gamification Ethics

- Tester Coins, Lucky Spin Wheel turns, and Mystery Box rewards are internal gamified incentives to celebrate active community members.
- **Zero Real-World Value:** Coins have no cash or monetary value and cannot be redeemed for real currency.
- Any attempt to manipulate the wheel spin odds, exploit network packet glitches, or farm coins via automated scripts will result in immediate coin balance forfeiture and account closure.
