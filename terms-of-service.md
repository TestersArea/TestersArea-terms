## 1. Description of Service

Testers Area is a platform that connects Android app developers to mutually test each other's apps on the Google Play Store. The service operates on a **swap-based model**: you test someone's app, and they test yours, for a 14-day period. The purpose is to help developers get genuine feedback and real-device testing from other developers.

## 4. Testing Rules & Fair Usage

### 4.1 Testing Commitment
When you accept or initiate a testing swap request, you commit to the following:
- Actively use and test the partner's app for the full **14-day** testing period.
- Use the partner's app for a minimum required duration each day (verified through Android Usage Statistics).
- Upload genuine, freshly-taken screenshots daily as proof of your testing activity.
- Keep the partner's app installed on your device for the entire testing period.

### 4.2 Prohibited Activities
The following activities are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate account penalties or permanent ban:

| Violation | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fake Testing** | Uploading old screenshots, screenshots from other devices, edited images, or screenshots not taken during the required time window |
| **App Uninstallation** | Uninstalling the partner's app during the active 14-day testing period |
| **Fraud** | Using automated tools, emulators, bots, or any method to fake app usage time or screenshots |
| **Multi-Accounting** | Creating multiple accounts on the same or different devices to abuse the system |
| **Harassment** | Sending abusive, threatening, or inappropriate messages through the in-app chat |
| **Spam** | Sending excessive, irrelevant, or unsolicited testing requests |
| **Clock Manipulation** | Changing your device clock to fake screenshot timestamps (detected via NTP verification) |
| **Impersonation** | Pretending to be another developer or submitting apps you do not own |

### 4.3 Reputation System
- Your **Reputation Score** reflects your testing reliability and honesty. It increases with completed honest testing and decreases with violations.
- **Golden Points** are earned by successfully completing full 14-day testing periods.
- Repeated or severe violations may result in automatic reputation penalties, temporary restrictions, or permanent account suspension.

### 4.4 Testing Verification
To ensure fairness, we use the following verification methods:
- **App Usage Tracking:** We verify your daily usage time for the partner's app using Android Usage Statistics.
- **Screenshot Timestamp Verification:** We verify the actual capture time of your screenshots using EXIF metadata and filename patterns.
- **Installation Detection:** We check whether the partner's app remains installed on your device.
- **Network Time Verification:** We use NTP (Network Time Protocol) to independently verify timestamps and prevent clock manipulation.
