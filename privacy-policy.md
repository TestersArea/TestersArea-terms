## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you sign in using Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0), we collect:
- **Email Address:** Used to identify your account, send testing updates, and authenticate your identity.
- **Display Name:** Your public developer name shown to matched testing partners and on your profile.
- **Profile Photo URL:** Your Google profile avatar, displayed within the App UI.
- **User ID:** An internal unique UUID generated by Supabase Auth to link your account data securely.

### 1.2 Device & Hardware Information
- **Android ID (SSAID):** We collect your device's unique 64-bit Android ID to enforce our strictly required one-account-per-device rule and prevent fraudulent multi-account abuse. Your Android ID is stored securely on private servers and is **never** shared with third parties or other users.
- **Device Model, Manufacturer & OS Version:** Collected to display device diagnostics on your profile and ensure technical compatibility with partner apps.

### 1.3 App Usage Data (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`)
- **Usage Statistics:** To verify that you have actively tested your partner's app for the required daily duration (minimum 2 minutes), our native Android service queries Android's `UsageStatsManager` / `UsageEvents` API.
- **Strict Scope Limitation:** We read usage data **only** for the specific package ID of the partner app you are currently assigned to test. We do **not** scan, collect, or log your general app usage history, personal browsing habits, or activity in other installed applications.
- **Prominent Disclosure:** This permission is requested with a prominent in-app disclosure dialog explaining its purpose before directing you to Android system settings.

### 1.4 Installed Apps Verification
- **Targeted Package Verification:** We query whether the assigned partner app is currently installed on your device to prevent uninstallation fraud during the active 14-day test cycle.
- **No Broad Scanning:** We do **not** collect, catalog, or transmit your full list of installed applications. The high-risk `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` permission is intentionally not requested.

### 1.5 Media & Screenshot Proofs
- **Screenshot Proofs (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** You select and upload screenshots as daily proof of testing.
  - Screenshots are compressed locally to WebP format on your device to minimize data consumption.
  - Uploaded proofs are stored in private cloud storage buckets protected by Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).
  - Screenshots are shared **only** with the specific developer whose app you are testing.
  - Proofs are **never** indexed, made public, or sold.
  - Proofs are **automatically deleted** from our servers after the testing cycle ends (maximum retention: 20 days).
- **EXIF Metadata (Capture Timestamps):** We extract capture timestamps from selected screenshot metadata to verify that screenshots were genuinely taken during the active daily testing window. We do **not** read or store GPS location data.

### 1.6 Push Notifications & Reminders
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token:** Collected to deliver notifications regarding testing swap requests, proof approvals, rejections, and streak reminders. Deleted upon account deletion.
- **Local Reminders:** Scheduled on-device reminders use standard Android notification channels (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) to remind you of pending daily tests. High-risk exact alarm permissions (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`) are **not** requested.

### 1.7 In-App Partner Chat & Feedback Data
- **Partner Chat:** Messages exchanged between matched testing partners are encrypted in transit, stored securely in our database, and visible only to the two participating developers. Chat logs are deleted when the swap finishes or when either developer deletes their account.
- **Bug Reports & User Feedback:** Text feedback and diagnostic screenshots submitted via `Send Feedback` or daily proof reviews are collected solely to assist testing partners and improve platform stability.

### 1.8 App Access Credentials (Demo Logins & Promo Codes)
- When a developer submits an app requiring authentication or a paid app, they may submit demo login credentials, test OTP instructions, or Google Play promo codes in the Access Notes.
- **Data Protection:** Access credentials are encrypted in transit, stored in private database rows, and displayed solely to the matched testing partner assigned to that specific app.
- **Developer Notice:** Developers are strictly cautioned **never** to provide personal credentials, real bank accounts, or production root passwords; only sandbox or dummy test accounts should be used.

### 1.9 Financial & Subscription Data (PRO Memberships)
- When you purchase a PRO Subscription, the transaction is processed directly through **Google Play In-App Billing** via RevenueCat (`purchases_flutter`).
- We receive your subscription status, product ID, transaction ID, and expiration date.
- We **never** collect, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or bank account details.

### 1.10 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)
For users on the free tier, we display advertisements provided by **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**:
- Google AdMob may automatically collect:
  - Google Advertising ID (AAID/GAID).
  - IP address (for approximate location to serve localized ads).
  - Device technical specifications and general diagnostic metrics.
  - Ad performance data (views, clicks, impressions).
- You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime via **Android Settings > Google > Ads**.
- Subscribing to PRO removes all third-party advertising SDK calls.

### 1.11 Developer Pro Tools (Local Execution)
- Utilities such as the **APK & App Bundle Analyzer** and **Screenshot Mockup Studio** operate **locally on your device**.
- APK files and promotional graphics analyzed with Pro Tools are processed strictly in local device RAM and are **never** transmitted to our external servers.

### 1.12 On-Device Local Data (SharedPreferences)
The following preferences remain strictly on your device and are never sent to our servers:
- App UI theme (Light/Dark mode).
- Selected language preference (8 languages supported: English, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Russian, Chinese, Korean).
- Local notification alert preferences and cached session states.

## 4. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous technical safeguards to protect your personal data:
- **Row Level Security (RLS):** Every database table in Supabase is governed by RLS policies ensuring users can only read and write their own authorized records.
- **End-to-End Encryption in Transit:** All client-server communication is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
- **Encrypted at Rest:** Database records and cloud storage buckets are encrypted on enterprise cloud infrastructure.
- **Zero Plaintext Credentials:** We use Google OAuth 2.0; we never handle or store user passwords.

## 5. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

### 5.1 Automated Data Cleanup
- **Testing Proofs (Screenshots):** Permanently deleted from our servers within 20 days after a testing swap completes.
- **Chat Logs:** Automatically removed following swap completion or partner account deletion.

### 5.2 User Account Deletion
You maintain complete ownership of your data and can permanently delete your account at any time via:  
**Settings > Delete Account**

Upon confirmation:
- Your user profile and authentication credentials are deleted immediately.
- All your submitted apps, testing requests, proofs, coin balances, notifications, and chat records are **permanently and irreversibly erased** from production storage.

## 6. Android Permissions & Justification

| Permission | Category | Purpose & Justification |
|---|---|---|
| `android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS` | Protected | Reads daily usage duration for the assigned partner app only (prominently disclosed prior to settings redirect). |

| `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Normal | Haptic feedback on notifications. |
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Normal | Communicating with backend APIs and servers. |

*Note: High-risk permissions such as `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, and `USE_EXACT_ALARM` are **NOT** used.*
